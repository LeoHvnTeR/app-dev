#  Series:  Money Heist 
Genre: Action, Crime, Drama

A Spanish television series created by Álex Pina. The plot revolves around a mysterious man known as the Professor, who recruits a group of eight criminals to carry out an ambitious plan to rob the Royal Mint of Spain, aiming to print billions of euros. The series is known for its intricate plot, character development, and emotional depth, making it one of the most popular non-English language series on Netflix.
